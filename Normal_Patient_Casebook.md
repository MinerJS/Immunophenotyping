NO IMMUNOPHENOTYPIC ABNORMALITY
Flow cytometry is a means of characterizing leukocyte populations. It can aid in the differential diagnosis of hematologically 
abnormal patients having, or suspected of having hematopoietic neoplasia including chronic leukemia, acute leukemia, non-
Hodgkin lymphoma, myeloma, myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS), and/or myeloproliferative neoplasms (MPN). Crucial to the 
identification of aberrant populations in these clinical situations is familiarity with normal cell populations present in whole blood, 
bone marrow and lymph node tissue samples. The following are examples of normal samples stained with ClearLLab 10C panels.
PERIPHERAL WHOLE BLOOD
Case #1: Normal Whole Blood
Clinical Vignette

immunophenotyping using ClearLLab 10C Panels.

Results of Flow Cytometric Immunophenotyping
Flow cytometric immunophenotyping identified no immunophenotypically aberrant populations in this case. No white 
cell abnormality is identified by morphology. Note that correlation with clinical, morphologic, and laboratory data is 
recommended, and that a malignant process cannot be ruled solely on the basis of this assay.
