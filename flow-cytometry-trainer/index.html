Flow Cytometry Simulator

i
Setup (Rolling): live preview only — events stream past continuously and nothing is saved. Use it to check gating/alignment first, like "Setup" on a real cytometer.
Acquire (Save): events are recorded into the dataset (up to 6000) and stay on the plots, like pressing "Acquire" for real.
i
Sets how tightly sheath fluid focuses the sample core stream through the laser.
Low: narrowest core, single-file cells, <0.1% doublets, tightest CVs — best resolution, slowest.
Medium: balanced core width, ~2% coincidence — typical routine setting.
High: widest core, faster throughput, but ~15% doublets/coincidence and broader CVs — resolution suffers.
i
Playback speed only — it doesn't change the sample, just how fast events are animated across the instrument and plots.
Slow Motion: watch one cell's pulse cross the laser at a time.
Normal: ~150 events/sec, close to a real acquisition's pace.
Fast / Instant: skip ahead to a filled-in dataset. Combined with High flow rate this triggers a coincidence/yield warning, just as it would on a real instrument.

CD4+ Helper T-Cell

Lymphoid Lineage / T-Cell

Description

CD4+ helper T cells are crucial mediators of adaptive immunity. They coordinate immune responses by secreting cytokines that stimulate B cells to produce antibodies and assist CD8+ cytotoxic T cells. In flow cytometry, they are identified as CD45+ (bright), CD3+, CD4+, and CD8-.

Flow-Gate Interactive Learning & Animation Suite

Step-by-step visual guides & live animated demonstrations for all flow cytometry modules

Live Animated Stream Simulation Laser & Pulse Active
Hydrodynamic Focusing & Laser Interrogation: Sheath fluid compresses the sample stream so single cells pass through the focal spots of 3 spatially separated lasers (Blue 488nm, Red 638nm, Violet 405nm).
Step 1

Select Tube Panel & Sample Case

Choose between QC Screening, T-Cell, B-Cell, or Myeloid panels. Select Normal Whole Blood or AML cases (M0 through M7).

Step 2

Adjust Flow Rate & Hydrodynamic Core

Switch between Low (10 µL/min) for tightest core stream (<0.1% doublets) vs High (60 µL/min) for fast throughput (higher doublet coincidence).

Step 3

Inspect Pulse Height, Area, & Width (FSC-A vs FSC-H)

Observe the digital signal processor oscilloscope. Singlets fall along the diagonal on FSC-A vs FSC-H; doublets fall below the diagonal due to increased time-of-flight.

Interactive Gating & Population Identification

Flow cytometry isolates specific leukocyte subsets through sequential bivariate dot plots. Events are color-coded based on gating precedence:

1. Singlet Gate (FSC_A vs FSC_H)

Excludes doublets and cell aggregates along the diagonal line.

2. Viable Cells Gate (FSC_A vs SSC_A)

Excludes dead cells and debris (low FSC events).

3. CD45 vs SSC Gating

Identifies Lymphocytes (Bright CD45, Low SSC), Monocytes, Granulocytes, and Blast Gate (CD45dim).
