CD4+ Helper T-Cell

Lymphoid Lineage / T-Cell

Description

CD4+ helper T cells are crucial mediators of adaptive immunity. They coordinate immune responses by secreting cytokines that stimulate B cells to produce antibodies and assist CD8+ cytotoxic T cells. In flow cytometry, they are identified as CD45+ (bright), CD3+, CD4+, and CD8-.
